# Printing and Bench-Assembly Notes

## Before printing

- Confirm that the speaker, power lead and connector match the intended model and that the measurements in the source suit your actual unit.
- All dimensions are in millimetres. Printer calibration, material shrinkage and slicer compensation can change fit.
- Treat this as a fit-test prototype only. Do not install it overhead.

## Parts to print

| Part | Quantity for one prototype | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| Ceiling plate | 1 | Bench-test only; mounting to a ceiling is not validated. |
| Upper ring | 1 | Inspect bayonet lugs and radial bridges closely. |
| Lower ring | 1 | Confirm the speaker sits without blocking openings. |
| Support arm | 3 | Check each arm for consistent dimensions and defects. |
| Locking pin | 6 | Test insertion and retention without forcing the parts. |
| Cable clip | As needed | Test with the actual cable; do not pinch or abrade insulation. |

## Initial print approach

These are starting points, not validated production settings:

- Use a common, well-calibrated FDM material and profile for the first fit trial.
- Use a moderate layer height suitable for your printer (for example, 0.20 mm) and inspect small holes and slots for dimensional accuracy.
- Use enough perimeters and infill for a robust prototype, but do not interpret these settings as proof of load capacity.
- Orient each part to minimise poor surface quality in the bayonet tracks and pin holes; review overhangs and supports in the slicer.
- Keep a record of material, printer, slicer, layer height, walls, infill, orientation and any support removal.
- Do not scale parts independently. If changing dimensions, edit the source parameters and record the change.

No particular filament, infill percentage, wall count, or print orientation has been validated for safe overhead service.

## Bench assembly sequence

1. Inspect all parts for cracks, voids, warping, stringing, sharp edges and incomplete layers. Reject damaged parts.
2. Test the upper ring's bayonet lugs in the ceiling plate slots by hand on a flat bench. Do not force the fit. Record whether entry and rotation are smooth, loose, or obstructed.
3. Check the upper and lower arm tabs against their sockets. Align holes and test pins gently. Do not hammer pins into place.
4. Repeat the arm fit with all three arms installed. Check for twist, uneven spacing and interference.
5. Place the lower ring on the speaker only for a careful fit check on a bench. Confirm that it does not obstruct speaker openings, buttons, ventilation, or the power lead.
6. Check the cable clip on the actual cable. It must not crush, sharply bend, scrape or put tension on the cable.
7. Test the whole assembly on a bench and document every loose joint, interference, or unintended release. Do not conduct a drop test over people or breakable objects.

## What is not covered

This guide does not specify ceiling fasteners, ceiling substrate, safe working load, fatigue life, fire performance, electrical safety, or a certified installation method. Those require independent engineering assessment and testing before any overhead use.
