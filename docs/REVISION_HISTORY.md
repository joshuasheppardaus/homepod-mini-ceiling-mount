# Revision History

## Revision 7 — initial public prototype package

This package is based on the Revision 7 OpenSCAD prototype supplied during the design collaboration, with targeted mesh/geometry changes described below.

- Added shallow radial bridges under the ceiling plate to connect the upper ring's bayonet lugs to the ring body, because the lug track radius is smaller than the ring's inner radius.
- Adjusted ring socket cuts to avoid tiny disconnected mesh slivers.
- Exported separate STL files for the six unique part types.
- Checked the six distributed STL meshes for watertightness and one connected component per file.

### Known limitations / not yet validated

- The complete assembly has not passed a physical dimensional fit test.
- Bayonet engagement and accidental unlocking resistance have not been validated.
- Arm and pin strength, long-term creep, fatigue, temperature effects and print-direction effects have not been tested.
- Ceiling attachment, fasteners, substrate and safe working load have not been engineered or certified.
- No claim is made that this prototype is safe for overhead installation.

Future changes should be recorded with a new revision number, date, concise description, and test evidence. Do not silently replace the Revision 7 files.
